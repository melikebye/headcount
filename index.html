Every day, childcare directors spend hours building handmade schedules that still miss state-required ratios, taking time away from the children, staff, and families they'd rather be serving.

Staffing made easy

Every state limits how many children one teacher can look after, and the limit applies in every room, all day long. Children arrive and leave at different times, teachers call out, and most directors still build the schedule by hand. Headcount reads the check-in and staff files a center already has, works out how many teachers the law requires in each room every 15 minutes, and places the center's own staff where the children are. When someone calls out, it repairs the day in seconds.

  1. Upload two files

    Children's check-ins and your staff's hours, straight from the software you already use.

  2. Headcount forecasts each room

    How many children, every 15 minutes, and how many teachers the law requires for them.

  3. Get the week's schedule

    Your own people, placed where the children are. Any short-staffed hour is flagged before it happens.

Built for directors
