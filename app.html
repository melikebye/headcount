Rooms and the rules applied to them

North Carolina rules in three categories: infants 1:5, toddlers 1:6, preschoolers 1:10. The state sets 1:10 at age 2, 1:15 at age 3 and 1:20 at age 4, and applies the youngest child's ratio to a mixed group, so one preschool category uses 1:10. Rooms in the same category may be combined before 8:30am and after 4pm when the combined group fits the maximum size. Breaks, staff qualifications and nap-time rules are not modeled yet. Check every rule against the current state code before relying on it.
